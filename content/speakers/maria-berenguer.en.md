{{<speaker_talk title="A disturbance in the Agile force">}}
Times have changed, and the waterfall empire is regaining ground. Agile teams, lost without clear leadership, are failing in their ways of working. I sense a disturbance in the force. AI advances unstoppably across the galaxy, while Agile teams, weakened by earlier battles, face a threat they do not yet understand.

The confederation of technology organisations has spent years trying to transform itself and adapt to new environments in this galaxy, where change is the only constant that prevails, with AI's permission. Do they succeed? As in every great battle, sometimes you win and sometimes you lose.

Agile is a mindset that is easy to understand and hard to apply. One of the weapons an Agile team has is eXtreme Programming, which gives us tools to train technical excellence, with its values and practices, and so awaken the force of teams, now that AI is revolutionising the way we develop software and putting our technical excellence to the test.

In this talk we will discover how XP goes beyond writing quality code, putting the experience of development teams first and unleashing their full potential.

I will share stories in which I have guided organisations that have started their journey: a path of discovering new Agile dimensions in teams, with their mistakes and successes. A transformation towards a culture of responsibility and collaboration in building products.

Get ready for a series of real cases that seem straight out of science fiction.
