{{<speaker_bio>}}

Visual effects artist, science communicator and film educator with more than 16 years of experience in the international industry. Throughout her career she has worked on 36 feature films and many film and television productions, combining her professional work with outreach and training on cinematography, technology and visual effects.

{{</speaker_bio>}}

{{<speaker_talk title="The evolution of technology in cinema: from the cinematograph to artificial intelligence">}}
From the invention of the cinematograph to the arrival of computers and artificial intelligence, the history of cinema has always been tied to the evolution of technology. In this talk we will go through some of the main advances that have transformed the way stories are created, filmed and told: from the first film tricks and optical effects to digital visual effects, the creation of impossible environments and characters, and the new tools based on artificial intelligence. A journey through more than a century of innovation to understand how technology has continuously pushed the limits of cinematic imagination.

{{</speaker_talk>}}
